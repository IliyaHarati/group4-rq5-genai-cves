02 / The evidence funnel

881 distinct CVEs were discovered before filtering by date; 880 product–CVE candidates fell within the publication window. The ledger records 791 confirmed, 70 unrelated, 0 rejected and 19 unresolved pairs.

NVD keywords and supplementary CPE-family queries discover candidates. Exact scoped affected-product metadata, vulnerable CPEs or exact upstream repository disclosures establish attribution. Linked disclosures must be retrievable and contain a CVE ID or product marker. Raw NVD responses, CNA records, disclosure snapshots in the compressed evidence archive and the audit ledger are retained.

03 / Severity coverage

NVD-authored CVSS 3.1 metrics take priority; otherwise Primary metrics and a deterministic source order are used. The exported summary also gives NVD-only counts. Unknown severity stays visible.

04 / Unresolved evidence

Bounds add all discovered unresolved candidates. They are not confidence intervals and cannot account for undiscovered CVEs.

06 / Interpretation

These are reporting counts in a purposively selected product set. Product age, popularity, deployment exposure, researcher attention, disclosure policies and component scope differ. No common exposure denominator is available. A larger bar therefore does not establish a higher vulnerability rate or lower intrinsic security.

NVD/CPE coverage and aliases can be incomplete, and automated verification can miss or misclassify cases. The publication cutoff is fixed; source records and metrics use the collection snapshot. EPSS, exploitation evidence and CVSS 4.0 are outside the primary analysis.
